Privacy Policy

Chip Chaser · Effective November 7, 2026

Chip Chaser doesn't collect, sell, or share personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking, and no third-party code that collects data.

Your collection

The chips you mark, your notes, and stores you add are saved on your iPhone. If you're signed in to iCloud, they're also saved in the private iCloud database of your own Apple ID so they sync across your devices. That data is stored by Apple under your account; the developer can't see or access it. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Location

If you allow it, the app uses your location only on your device, to sort dealers by distance and center the map near you. Your location is never stored or sent anywhere. You can turn it off any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services.

Backups and sharing

When you export a backup or share your missing or extras list, the app creates a file or text and hands it to the destination you pick (Files, AirDrop, Messages, and so on). Nothing is sent unless you choose to.

Links to other apps

Tapping a dealer's website, phone number, or directions opens Safari, Phone, or Maps. Those are governed by their own privacy policies.

Children

Chip Chaser doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
